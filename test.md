# talhabilaldev Policy Q&A Test Set

Source documents: **Discount Policy** and **Return Policy** (effective October 06, 2026).

- **Relevant (1-10):** fully answerable from the documents.
- **Partially relevant (11-20):** about the store, but the documents only cover part of the answer.
- **Irrelevant (21-30):** unrelated to the store. For a support chatbot, the ideal reply is a polite out-of-scope response.

---

## Relevant (10)

### 1. How many days do I have to return an item?

**Answer:** 14 days from the delivery date.
**Source:** Return Policy, section 1

### 2. Who pays return shipping if I just change my mind?

**Answer:** The customer pays. talhabilaldev only pays when the item is defective, damaged, or incorrect.
**Source:** Return Policy, section 8

### 3. How much is the first order discount?

**Answer:** 8% off with a welcome code, up to a maximum of $40. Each customer can use it once.
**Source:** Discount Policy, sections 1 and 6

### 4. Can I use a student code and a newsletter code on the same order?

**Answer:** No. Student, first order, and newsletter codes cannot be combined with each other. Only one promo code is allowed per order.
**Source:** Discount Policy, section 4

### 5. My phone arrived damaged. What should I do?

**Answer:** Contact support within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos or a short video. You can choose a free replacement or a full refund, including original shipping costs.
**Source:** Return Policy, section 4

### 6. How much do I pay for five USB-C chargers at $20 each?

**Answer:** $95.00. Five units fall in the 3 to 5 unit tier (5% off), so $100 minus $5.
**Source:** Discount Policy, sections 2 and 3

### 7. How long does a refund take?

**Answer:** 5 to 10 business days after the return is received and inspected. It goes back to your original payment method.
**Source:** Return Policy, section 7

### 8. What is the maximum combined discount if I stack seasonal, bundle, and bulk discounts?

**Answer:** 25% off.
**Source:** Discount Policy, section 4

### 9. How does the referral reward work?

**Answer:** You and your friend each get a $10 credit when the friend places a first order of $100 or more. Credits expire 90 days after they are issued.
**Source:** Discount Policy, sections 1 and 6

### 10. Can I return headphones if I already broke the hygiene seal?

**Answer:** No. Earbuds and headphones with broken hygiene seals cannot be returned.
**Source:** Return Policy, section 3

## Partially Relevant (10)

### 11. I bought a laptop in the Black Friday sale and want to return it opened. How much will I get back?

**Covered:** Refunds are for the amount actually paid, not the list price. Opened items in like-new condition get a refund minus a restocking fee of up to 10%.
**Not covered:** Whether the fee is calculated on the sale price or the list price.
**Source:** Discount Policy section 8; Return Policy section 2

### 12. Can I swap my phone for a different model?

**Covered:** There is no direct swap. You return the original item and place a new order.
**Not covered:** How price differences are handled, or whether the new order can get a discount code.
**Source:** Return Policy, section 9

### 13. What is the exact restocking fee on an opened laptop?

**Covered:** Up to 10%.
**Not covered:** The exact percentage. The documents don't say what decides it.
**Source:** Return Policy, section 2

### 14. I live outside the country. Can I return an item from abroad?

**Covered:** You must ship to the address provided, using a tracked service, with the RA number.
**Not covered:** International shipping, international return eligibility, and customs costs.
**Source:** Return Policy, section 5

### 15. My tablet stopped working after 3 months. What are my options?

**Covered:** That is outside the 14-day return window and the 7-day defect window. All products have a manufacturer's warranty, and talhabilaldev will help you start a warranty claim.
**Not covered:** How long the warranty lasts or what it covers.
**Source:** Return Policy, section 4

### 16. I want to order 15 monitors. What discount do I get and how do I order?

**Covered:** 11 or more units get 12% off, and you should contact us for a custom quote (support@talhabilaldev.com).
**Not covered:** Quote turnaround time, delivery terms, and payment terms.
**Source:** Discount Policy, section 2

### 17. If I return only the case from a laptop bundle, how much is refunded?

**Covered:** You get the amount actually paid for the returned item, and the bundle discount may be recalculated on the remaining items.
**Not covered:** The recalculation method, or whether the 10% bundle discount is lost entirely.
**Source:** Discount Policy, section 8

### 18. How long does it take to get my Return Authorization (RA) number?

**Covered:** You email support with your order number and reason, then wait for approval and the RA number.
**Not covered:** Any approval timeline. Support hours are Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 6 PM.
**Source:** Return Policy, sections 5 and 10

### 19. The price dropped 3 days after I bought an item during the Eid sale. Can I get the difference back?

**Covered:** Price adjustments are available within 7 days of the order, but not for flash sales, clearance items, or limited-time promo events.
**Not covered:** Whether a seasonal event like Eid counts as a "limited-time promo event".
**Source:** Discount Policy, sections 1 and 9

### 20. Can I use my student discount on a newly launched phone?

**Covered:** Student discounts are 5% off eligible products. New launches are excluded for their first 30 days.
**Not covered:** Which products are "eligible", or any other promotion for new launches.
**Source:** Discount Policy, sections 1 and 5
